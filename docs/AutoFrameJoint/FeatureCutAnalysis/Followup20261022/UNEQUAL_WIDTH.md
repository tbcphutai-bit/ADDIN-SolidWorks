# Unequal primary profile widths

## Definition and evidence

Widths below are lengths of the identified primary straight base-profile Sketch segments. The raw segments, thickness and bend radii were read by API; interpreting a segment as primary Web is a separate domain interpretation. Overall body footprints, return-lip extensions and nominal labels are not interchangeable with this width. The old longest-segment `WEB` label alone cannot classify arbitrary profiles.

| 1618 fr1 × fr2 | 75 × 65 mm | Archived original replay had zero difference at its exact pose. The later stock-copy boundary-witness BUTT run now passed two native Cuts, zero body difference/overlap and a 0.10 mm gap; the long Cut retained reported exposed area. |
| 1618 fr1 × fr4 | 75 × 65 mm | Same unequal primary widths, with archived exact-pose zero-difference replay. It does not qualify a generalized rule by itself. |
| 2354 fr02 × fr01 | 55 × 70 mm | New folded BUTT solver and two native Cuts passed on copies, zero body differences/overlap, 0.10 mm gap and reported exposed area. This is actual unequal-width solver evidence. |
| 1618 fr3 × fr4 | 65 × 65 mm | Stock-copy solver and native writer passed, but this is equal primary width. Different return footprints must not be reported as unequal Webs. |
| 3615 T1/T2 pairs | 50 × 50 mm | Recorded primary segments are equal despite different return footprints. |
| 2354 fr04 × fr03 | 70 × 70 mm | Primary widths are equal; different Wall heights do not establish unequal Web widths. |

The 1618 fr1 profile has an inclined outer Wall. A solver restricted to perpendicular parallel Walls cannot accept it merely because the primary widths are known. The current candidate dispatch has been extended to consider every ordered segment, including inclined strips, rather than inferring their direction from an image. The completed source audit records the committed candidate identity in [SOURCE_VERIFICATION.json](SOURCE_VERIFICATION.json); the installed DLL was not rebuilt.

## Qualification boundary

The folded BUTT strategy does not require equal primary widths. It derives its two physical support planes independently, then validates every native paired skin, bend and required KEEP stock. The 2354 55/70 mm and 1618 fr1/fr2 75/65 mm pairs are actual unequal-width positive tests in this snapshot. It does not prove universal miter behavior or complete concealment.

The first current 1618 fr1/fr2 stock native test **FAILED** its planar-support guard and called no writer. The subsequent `native1618_fr1fr2_stock_boundary_01` **PASSED** using the true native extreme with trimmed topology witnesses: one planar support and a second boundary supported by two native vertices with no planar support Face. It created two Cuts, Sketch status 3/3, 22/16 relations, zero dimensions/planes, zero body difference and a 0.10 mm gap. The long cap measured 240.645027 mm² total and 23.117370 mm² exposed at the defined probe section. The source boundary branch for an edge witness was not exercised by this vertex-supported pass. The earlier failure remains in the matrix. The fr1/fr4 pair is still unqualified for the new rule. Archived named Cut operations are research evidence and are not replayed as the general solver.

Older analytic U-family runtime variants included unequal widths 75/55, 45/65, 90/70 and 80/60 mm. They are synthetic tests and must not be described as tests of these actual 1618 Parts.

No result here establishes full edge concealment for every unequal-width profile. A qualified BUTT result may explicitly report exposed area under the user's accepted policy. It remains distinct from reproducing the original miter cuts.
