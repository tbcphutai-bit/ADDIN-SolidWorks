## Geometry and manufacturing scope

- The folded BUTT strategy permits reported exposure. Its 2354 result leaves about 29.978 mm² exposed at the defined long-Cut probe section.
- The probe is a finite 0.01 mm slab through the short sheet's midpoint. It measures section coverage, not complete visual occlusion from all directions.
- Every claimed valid result still requires one remaining valid solid, exact gap/overlap checks and required KEEP checks. Unsupported chains/skins, ambiguous ends, bad samples and missing material are refused.
- A small successful sample set does not qualify all 1618/3615 cases, arbitrary angles, scaled/mirrored placements, assemblies with reused Parts or multiple configuration scopes.
- Flat-pattern/unfold/refold and downstream manufacturing checks are `NOT_RUN` for the new writer.

## 3615 scope qualification

T1 stock-copy application passed with four actual native Cuts, a 0.10 mm gap and zero body difference using the old U `FIXED_PLANE_PRISM` writer. Its Sketch counters are -1 (`NOT_AVAILABLE`), not evidence of fully constrained face-attached Sketches. The new writer requirement remains unmet for this path.

The final 2354 fr04/fr03 native regression also passed four legacy U/Taller-U Cuts with zero body difference and a 0.10 mm gap. Its fixed-plane Sketch representation still does not qualify the new face-attached/direct-relation requirement.

## Native session faults

Native runs record their own SOLIDWORKS revision: both 32.5.0 and 33.5.0 sessions were used. Do not assign one revision to all rows or turn a later session fault into a failure of already completed body comparisons. A fresh session was started for the next pending boundary-width test. The new boundary-support run later passed the 75/65 mm inclined stock-copy pair with planar and vertex witnesses. This does not exercise the edge-only branch or all profile/placement variants.

A later fresh-session stale-ticket test passed the expected refusal: apply returned false with `PREVIEW_STALE`, zero Cuts and unchanged original bodies. Its report gap/interference values were unmeasured defaults because refusal preceded the writer; they are not evidence of an actual zero gap. The solver candidate alone had a 0.10 mm gap and zero overlap. The earlier RPC failure remains a separate row.

## Performance and process exit

The new native runs took tens of seconds for initial solve and apply. The real public-manager sequence reused its cache for three subsecond repeat requests, but its first public solve took 37.369 seconds. Smooth installed interaction and initial latency remain unverified. Supplied `0xffffffff` process exits lack a causal native stack; do not label them as proof of a particular solver or file defect.
